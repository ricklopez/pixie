01 / Foundations

Quiet surfaces

Charcoal establishes the workspace. Layered surfaces separate navigation, content, and inspection without heavy shadows.

Purposeful lime

Lime identifies a primary action, selection, or active execution. Success has its own color and an explicit label.

Evidence before spectacle

Motion follows recorded events. The graph explains what happened; it never implies verification that has not occurred.

Color roles

Typography & rhythm

DM Sans / interface

16px body · 14px controls and tables · 12px secondary metadata. Page titles use 32px; panel titles use 18–20px.

Reserve the website’s oversized typography for onboarding or brand moments. Keep app letter spacing natural.

Make recording state explicit. Separate the browser viewport from captured steps. Always offer a clear stop action.

API explorer

Keep method, endpoint, revision, inputs, and results together. Separate returned HTTP status from accepted business outcome.

Decision inspector

04 / Dust with a purpose

Brand moments

Particles may gather into the wordmark on welcome screens and the website. Keep them behind content and provide a pause control.

Execution flow

A short pulse follows an actual event along a graph edge. A running state may repeat subtly; a completed step becomes still.

Working surfaces

No ambient dust beneath code, forms, tables, or evidence. Reading and editing should not compete with motion.

120ms hover/focus · 200ms panel transitions · up to 400ms one-shot reveals. Honor reduced motion with static states, stop background rendering when hidden, and never use animation alone to communicate progress.
